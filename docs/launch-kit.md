## Product Hunt 草稿

Tagline: Live subtitles for Japanese audio in Chrome

Description:

Kotoba Live turns Japanese audio from your selected Chrome tab into live translated subtitles. Keep the original voice, move and resize the subtitle overlay, and choose whether to show Japanese text alongside the translation. Try it with 20 free minutes; no card required. Currently installed from a ZIP using Chrome's developer mode.

Maker comment:

Hi everyone — I'm building Kotoba Live for people who want to follow Japanese lessons, interviews, and livestreams that don't have usable translated captions.

Start playback, choose a translation language, and turn on subtitles for that tab. You can move the overlay, resize it without changing font size, and show or hide the Japanese original.

This is live speech recognition and translation, so noise, overlapping speech, and network conditions can affect accuracy and delay. It generates text subtitles; it doesn't replace the speaker's voice. I'm especially interested in feedback on subtitle readability and the first-use setup.

The free trial includes 20 minutes. Installation currently requires downloading and unpacking the extension ZIP. Chrome Web Store availability will be announced after approval.
