## Short description

Live translated subtitles for Japanese audio in Chrome, with optional Japanese text and movable, resizable captions.

## Description

Kotoba Live is a desktop Chrome extension that turns Japanese audio from a user-selected browser tab into translated subtitles, including English and Chinese. It can generate captions from audio even when a video has no existing subtitle track, while keeping the original sound playing.

Users can show or hide the Japanese original, move and resize the subtitle box, adjust its background, and distinguish speakers using numbers and colors. The extension also displays remaining translation time.

It is designed for following Japanese talks, interviews, lessons, and livestreams. Speech recognition and translation require an internet connection and cloud processing. Noise, overlapping speech, and network conditions can affect accuracy and delay. Some protected pages and players may not work.

New accounts receive a one-time 20-minute trial without a payment card. Membership costs USD 19.99 per month for 20 hours. A non-renewing USD 9.99 top-up provides 10 hours, valid for 90 days.

Installation currently uses a downloadable ZIP loaded through Chrome's developer mode, with instructions on the official website.
